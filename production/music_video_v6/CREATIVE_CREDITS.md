# Nobody Owns Tomorrow — creative and production credits

**Creator / lead director: Paul Carver Tiffany III.** Original song concept, artistic intent, musical/video direction, editorial corrections, narrative continuity, camera choreography, publishing decisions.

**Creative and technical collaborator: ChatGPT 6 (OpenAI).** Co-development of musical and visual interpretation, lyrics/direction, shot/storyboard design, custom Python animation renderer, song synchronization and provenance. This credits an AI collaborator, not a separate human owner.

**Song source: [Suno — Nobody Owns Tomorrow](https://suno.com/song/cf3d6b12-d528-4193-a8f0-7f4e9b997ea3).** Original Suno-created recording, clip ID `cf3d6b12-d528-4193-a8f0-7f4e9b997ea3`.

**Suno MCP integration:** Connected tool used to retrieve song metadata, audio analysis and lyric alignment. Current readback confirms 432 beats, 15 acoustic sections and 362 machine-aligned word entries. Those are machine observations, not human-certified perfect timing. Suno MCP is a hosted creative/analysis integration, **not an open-source Git submodule**.

**Cover / thumbnail:** **ChatGPT 6 with OpenAI image generation**, under Paul Carver Tiffany III's direction. The original cover illustration and optimized social-preview derivative were made in the creative session. They are **separate promotional images**, not the means by which the film's 2.5D animated frames were rendered. [Asset evidence](ART_ASSET_PROVENANCE.json).

**In-video artwork, karaoke, perspective camera and sprites:** Original procedural graphics/code made collaboratively by Paul Carver Tiffany III and ChatGPT 6 using [Pillow](https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow), [NumPy](https://github.com/numpy/numpy), [FFmpeg](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg) and DejaVu fonts. Of those, Pillow and FFmpeg are **the two actively used, independently developed upstream Git submodules required by Artilect**. Installed NumPy is credited as an additional dependency.

**Source interview:** [The Beyond Tomorrow Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NXITvkHIV9o). Inspiration and timestamped thematic references, not sampled footage, panelist collaboration, endorsement, or direct quotation of song lyrics.

**Not executed in V6:** ELYSY and UseSuno MV Studio. Their abandoned experimental pathway is archived under `production/LEGACY_PRE_V6_WORKFLOW.md`, and their Gitlink pins can be inspected in past commits, but they are **not current production technologies**.

Suno commercial recording entitlement and trademark usage in generated promotional art require creator review before commercial distribution.
