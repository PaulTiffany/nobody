# Nobody Owns Tomorrow — cover art and channel-branding direction

## Primary original-IP image (song cover / YouTube thumbnail)
An original, story-consistent scene: a dreaming child beside a luminous pegboard toy, with an impossible crowd of tiny multicolored light-sprite dancers emerging from the glowing board. The camera discovers a fantastic little dimensional doorway within one light peg. Beyond the bedroom window, neighboring rooms glow in different colors. Pop-cinematic, warm, playful, memorable at thumbnail size.

## Avoid
Official Lite-Brite trademarks, third-party logos, celebrity/speaker portraits, uncanny photorealistic impersonations, interview footage, and inaccurate claims of endorsement. Do not use the podcast thumbnail as our artwork. Avoid overlaid section-marker text.

## Typography and production needs
Master artwork as original IP, ideally at high resolution. Square song-cover crop plus 16:9 thumbnail-safe crop; the YouTube variant may add a short, large, legible title: **NOBODY OWNS TOMORROW**. Preserve central story elements in both crops and keep text out of the crucial visual focal point. Review rights and any generated-image licensing before public distribution.
